Shipping Policy

This policy outlines shipping timelines and delivery conditions.

1. Shipping Locations
- We currently ship within India only.
- International shipping is not supported.

2. Shipping Time
- Orders are processed within 1 to 2 business days.
- Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days after dispatch.

3. Shipping Charges
- Shipping charges are calculated at checkout.
- Free shipping is available on orders above ₹999.

4. Delays
- Delivery delays may occur due to unforeseen circumstances such as weather or logistics issues.

5. Tracking
- Customers will receive a tracking link once the order is shipped.
