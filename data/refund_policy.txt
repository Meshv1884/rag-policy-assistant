Refund Policy

We offer refunds for eligible products under the following conditions.

1. Eligibility for Refund
- Products must be returned within 7 days of delivery.
- Items must be unused, undamaged, and in original packaging.
- Proof of purchase is required.

2. Non-Refundable Items
- Digital products
- Gift cards
- Items marked as non-refundable at the time of purchase

3. Refund Process
- Once the return is received and inspected, the refund will be initiated.
- Refunds are processed to the original payment method.

4. Refund Timeline
- Refunds are typically processed within 5 to 7 business days after approval.

5. Partial Refunds
- Partial refunds may be granted for items with minor defects or missing packaging.
