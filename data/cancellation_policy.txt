Cancellation Policy

Customers may cancel their orders according to the rules below.

1. Order Cancellation
- Orders can be cancelled within 24 hours of placing the order.
- Orders that have already been shipped cannot be cancelled.

2. Cancellation Process
- To cancel an order, customers must contact customer support.
- A confirmation email will be sent once the cancellation is successful.

3. Refund on Cancellation
- If the order is cancelled before shipment, a full refund will be issued.
- If cancellation is requested after shipment, the refund policy will apply.

4. Exceptions
- Custom-made or personalized products cannot be cancelled once production has started.
